Minicut Shot Detector

Splits a mini cut into its shots — one file per shot, in the format you gave it, with every cut checked against the source.

Choose a source below and Inspect it. Then Analyse it, mark where each shot starts by playing through it, and split.

What this version handles

  • Hard cuts only. Dissolves, fades and wipes are not detected as edits.
  • Constant frame rate only. Variable frame rate sources are refused — re-encode to constant frame rate first.
  • Masking is reported, not applied. Letterboxing is measured and shown, but shots are always cut at full frame.
  • h264 and h265 only. Shots come back in the codec and container they went in as, re-encoded once so cuts land on the exact frame.

        Names shots against your shot list — describes each shot in a folder, matches it to a shot number, and renames the files once you approve.

        Works on any folder of single-shot files, whether or not the Splitter made them. Load your production knowledge and a shot list, point it at the folder, and review what it proposes.

        What this version handles

        • A model does the describing. Unlike the Splitter, this cannot be deterministic — the same shot can be described differently twice, which is why you review every row before anything is renamed.
        • It proposes, you decide. A shot it cannot place is left unnamed rather than guessed at, and nothing is renamed until you approve it.
        • One folder at a time. Shots are read from a single directory, not gathered from several.
        • Renaming appends. The original filename is kept and the shot number added to it, so a file can always be traced back to the mini cut it came from.
